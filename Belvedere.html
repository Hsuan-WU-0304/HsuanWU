Belvedere Vlorë

Complex Housing Models & Material Prototyping

How can architectural geometry be translated into a fabricable structural system?

Belvedere Vlorë explores the translation of eight hillside residential buildings into a 1:50 structural model. Beginning with the abstraction of the original building system into walls, beams, and slabs, a prototype was developed to test structural logic, material behavior, and edge conditions using resin-coated paperboard. The workflow was then translated from Revit to Rhino, where building components were reconstructed according to physical material thickness and developed into fabrication drawings. The resulting system was applied across all eight buildings and assembled with a 2.2 × 4.41 m topographic model, transforming complex digital geometry into a repeatable process of physical fabrication.

Structural Abstraction

From Building System to Structural Grid. The Belvedere Vlorë complex consists of eight residential buildings composed of modular prefabricated units. Structural walls, slabs, and column positions were extracted from the original floor plans and reconstructed as a simplified system for physical fabrication. This abstraction established an interlocking central structure with four perimeter columns, creating a repeatable structural logic that could be adapted across the eight buildings.

Testing Material and Edge Behavior through Paperboard and Resin

The abstracted structural system was first tested through a series of 1:50 material prototypes using paperboard and resin. Different edge conditions and resin applications were examined to understand how the material would deform, stiffen, and maintain the intended geometry. Through comparison between flat and rolled-edge prototypes, the flat-edge detail was selected as the final fabrication method and subsequently applied to the production of all eight building models.

Fabrication Strategy

From a Single Structural System to Eight Building Variations. A repeatable fabrication system was developed to translate different building geometries into physical models. Interlocking structural walls established the primary framework for slab assembly, allowing each building to be fabricated independently through a consistent process.

Serial Fabrication & Integration

The fabrication system was applied across all eight buildings, with each model produced and assembled independently. Once completed, the buildings were sequentially positioned within the large-scale topographic model to form the complete complex.
