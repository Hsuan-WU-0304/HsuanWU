BRILANT Hotels in Qerret

Complex Housing Models & Digital Fabrication

How can complex architectural geometry be translated into a precise physical model?

The model was developed as a system of individually fabricated components, translating complex floor plates, vertical elements, and connections into an assembly sequence. Precision in alignment and fabrication was essential to maintaining the spatial relationships between the interconnected volumes.

From BIM to Fabrication

Using a BIM-to-FAB workflow with Rhino.Inside.Revit, I extracted the project geometry and model information from the BIM environment into Rhino for physical model production. The digital geometry was then rationalized into a buildable structural system, where I designed the model’s supporting structure, resolved connections between components, and prepared CNC cutting drawings for fabrication. The resulting parts were precisely fabricated and assembled, translating the complex BIM model into a physical construction system while preserving the spatial relationships between slabs, vertical elements, and interconnected volumes.

Fabrication & Assembly

Following CNC fabrication, the model was assembled progressively from individual floor plates and structural components. Each volume was constructed as a precise three-dimensional framework, requiring continuous adjustment of alignment, connections, and tolerances during assembly. The individual structures were then integrated into the complete model, together with the terrain and landscape base.
