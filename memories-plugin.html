Memories Plug-In

Magical Mystery Tour

How can fragments of an ordinary journey be reassembled into an extraordinary spatial experience?

Along the way, fragments of the city were captured through 3D scanning, recording our immediate perceptions and shared experiences. Selected fragments were then cut, displaced, and collaboratively reassembled into an imaginary urban landscape—transforming an ordinary journey into a continuously reconstructed city of collective memory.

A Journey Without a Destination: Cut-Up

This is a journey without a destination. Along the way to the site, we used 3D scanning to capture our immediate perceptions and experiences. These fragments of a shared journey were then collected, selected, and reassembled into a new spatial composition.
