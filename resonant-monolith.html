Resonant Monolith

Morphogenesis through Material and Resonance

How can material behavior generate architectural form?

Resonant Monolith explores architecture as a process of formation rather than a predetermined object. Through material experimentation, physical modeling, digital reconstruction, and architectural translation, the project investigates how form can emerge through interactions between material behavior, geometry, and landscape.
